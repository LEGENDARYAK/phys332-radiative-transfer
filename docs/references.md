# Papers and relationship to this repository

1. Bethell, T. J., Zweibel, E. G., Heitsch, F., & Mathis, J. S. (2004).
   *Dust Heating by the Interstellar Radiation Field in Models of Turbulent
   Molecular Clouds.* Astrophysical Journal, 610, 801–812.
   - Appendix A: reverse photon trajectories, HG angles, exp(-tau_abs)
     weights, and angular averaging. The skeleton implements the basic
     elastic, grey, isotropically illuminated approach (not the paper's
     complete dust physics and benchmark suite).
   - Section 3.2: target for future homogeneous-cloud scattering validation.
2. Mathis, J. S., Whitney, B. A., & Wood, K. (2002).
   *Can Reflection from Grains Diagnose the Albedo?*
   Astrophysical Journal, 574, 812–821.
   - Section 2: hierarchical cloud construction (TODO).
   - Uses forward Monte Carlo from a central star; not the same source
     geometry as the Bethell external isotropic field.
3. PHYS 332 project proposal (user-provided Markdown).
   - Discusses gamma rays, Geant4, and forward-vs-reverse comparisons.
     This is a different physics model; explicitly separated in the scaffold.
